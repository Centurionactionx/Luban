Executive Committee

Outreach & Partnership Coordinator

Outreach coordinators are the voice of Luban. You will lead the effort to scale our impact by connecting high schoolers, teachers, student networks, and industry sponsors. This role is highly collaborative and crucial for reaching students in underserved communities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform school-level outreach to counselors, teachers, and business clubs to promote Luban competitions.
  • Establish and maintain relationships with guest speakers, mentors, and prospective judges from the business sector.
  • Support social media campaigns and newsletter communications to drive student engagement.
  • Collaborate with local community centers to coordinate outreach in underserved areas.

What We're Looking For

  • Excellent communication, writing, and presentation skills.
  • Confidence in sending outreach emails and speaking to professionals and peers.
  • Self-guided motivation, reliability, and structured organization skills.
  • Experience with social media coordination or community building is a strong plus.
  • Time commitment: Approx. 3–5 hours per week.
Executive Committee

Event Planning & Operations Specialist

Event Planning specialists design, construct, and execute Luban's core educational experiences. From our nationwide Stock Pitch Competitions to interactive business simulations, you will lead event design, operations, rules, and participant coordination.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design schedules, structural rules, cases, and grading rubrics for case competitions.
  • Manage logistics on the day of the event, including Zoom coordination, slides, breakout rooms, and presentation queues.
  • Create and structure resource documents (guides, templates) to support competitors.
  • Manage competitor registration data and coordinate communication regarding schedules and timeslots.

What We're Looking For

  • Exceptional attention to detail and ability to organize complex workflows.
  • Proactive mindset, creative thinking, and aptitude for designing games or simulations.
  • Experience with spreadsheet tools (Excel/Sheets) and video call platforms (Zoom/Teams).
  • Prior involvement in case competitions, debate, or school club planning is highly valued.
  • Time commitment: Approx. 3–5 hours per week.
