Privacy policy

Luban is a youth-led nonprofit in Canada that runs free educational events for students. This page covers the personal information we collect through lubancanada.com and through the Luban Market Lab at sim.lubancanada.com, what we do with it, who else sees it, and how to ask us to fix or delete it. If something here doesn't make sense, email us at jsun.luban@gmail.com.

What we collect

On lubancanada.com

The website has no accounts, analytics or advertising. If you use the contact form, we get the name, email address and message you type into it.

In the Market Lab

To set up your account we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with our sign-in provider. You can change the name other players see at any time.

While you take part, the simulator records your team and role, the orders you place, your trades, your watchlist, your team's cash and holdings, and its portfolio value over time. It also keeps a log of account changes, for example when someone accepts an invitation or a host moves a player to another team. If you send feedback from inside the Market Lab, we store your message along with your account and team so we can follow up.

People you invite

Hosts and organizers can add a student's email address to a team before that student signs up, so the invitation reaches the right person. Please only give us someone's email address if they're expecting to hear from us.

Technical logs

Our hosting providers automatically keep short-lived logs that include IP addresses and browser details. They're used to keep the services running and to spot abuse.

How we use it

  • Running the competition: creating your account, placing you on a team, processing simulated trades and ranking teams.
  • Emailing you about the event, such as information session dates and results. We don't send marketing emails.
  • Answering messages and feedback you send us.
  • Paying prizes. If your team wins, we'll email you to arrange payment and ask only for what we need to send it. We use those details for the prize and nothing else.
  • Keeping the simulator fair and secure, which includes looking into suspected cheating.
  • Fixing bugs and improving the Market Lab for future events.

We may share overall numbers that can't identify anyone, like how many students took part or how many trades were placed. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. If we ever want to use your information for something this page doesn't cover, we'll ask you first.

Who can see it

Your teammates can see your name, whether you're online, and the trades you make. Every participant can see team names, portfolio values and rankings on the leaderboard. Luban organizers and competition hosts can see accounts and trading history because they run the event. Only Luban organizers read feedback.

These companies handle information for us:

  • Clerk runs sign-in and account management.
  • Supabase hosts the database that stores simulator data.
  • Vercel hosts both websites.
  • EmailJS delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, and the fonts on lubancanada.com. Loading those fonts sends your IP address to Google.

Each of them uses your information to provide its service to us and has its own privacy policy. Stock, ETF and crypto prices come from Yahoo Finance. We don't send Yahoo any information about you.

Most of these providers are based in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed there, where US law applies to it.

We'll also hand over information when the law requires it, such as under a court order, or when we believe in good faith that someone's safety is at risk.

Cookies

The Market Lab uses cookies to keep you signed in. The site doesn't work without them, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies. lubancanada.com doesn't set any cookies. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, but you'll be signed out of the Market Lab if you do.

Keeping it safe

Both sites only load over encrypted connections. Clerk handles passwords and sign-in, so we never see your password. Inside the Market Lab, the tools that show other people's accounts are limited to organizers and hosts. No system is perfectly secure, though. If we learn of a breach that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we'll tell you and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Students under 18

Many of our participants are high school students. If you're under 18, talk to a parent or guardian before you sign up. A parent or guardian can make any of the requests in the next section on their child's behalf, including asking us to delete the account. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

How long we keep it

We keep Market Lab data, feedback included, for one year after the 2026 simulation ends, in case questions come up about results or prizes. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you. Contact form messages stay in our inbox until we no longer need them to help you.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email jsun.luban@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also delete your Market Lab account yourself at sim.lubancanada.com/delete-account.

Deleting your Market Lab account removes your name and email address. If you've already traded, those trades stay with your team so its results remain accurate, but they're no longer linked to you. Once your account is deleted you can't keep playing.

If you'd rather not get event emails, reply to one and tell us. We'll still contact you about things you need to know, like a prize your team won.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we'll email participants before it applies.

Contact

Send privacy questions and requests to jsun.luban@gmail.com.
