Start with the basics. Finish with a portfolio.

Any level of skill is welcome. The month runs in three parts, and the step-by-step guide shows where every button in the Market Lab is and what it does.

  1. Learn

    Resources and lessons cover the basics of managing a portfolio and trading. Weekly info sessions bring in guest speakers.

  2. Trade

    Your team starts with $100,000 in simulated cash and trades stocks, ETFs and crypto in the Market Lab until November 14.

  3. Compete

    When the simulation closes, the three teams with the highest portfolio values win cash prizes.

2026 schedule

Registration is closed. The simulation runs online for a month, from October 14 to November 14. Weekly information sessions happen during the event, and we'll send session dates to participants by email.

Cash prizes for the top three teams

The three teams with the highest portfolio values when the simulation closes win cash prizes, split evenly among each team's members. The full terms are in the contest rules.

  1. First place

    $500 cash prize, split among team members.

  2. Second place

    $200 cash prize, split among team members.

  3. Third place

    $100 cash prize, split among team members.
