Learn the basics, then put them into practice

Any level of skill is welcome. Luban provides resources and lessons covering the basics of managing a portfolio and trading, plus weekly information sessions with guest speakers.

  • Manage a portfolio. Practise with a stock portfolio through the October 1 to November 1 simulation window.
  • Build trading fundamentals. Learn the basics of trading through the resources and lessons provided as part of the event.
  • Join weekly info sessions. Learn from the event’s guest speakers throughout the simulation.
  • Start at any skill level. Individual and team entries begin with the same amount of cash.

Who can join?

You need to be a high school or university student. You don't need any trading experience.

Eligibility

Cash prizes for the top three teams

The three teams with the highest portfolio values when the simulation closes win cash prizes, split evenly among each team's members. The full terms are in the contest rules.

  1. First place

    $500 cash prize, split among team members.

  2. Second place

    $200 cash prize, split among team members.

  3. Third place

    $100 cash prize, split among team members.

Participation FAQ

Questions about entry, team matching, learning support, and registration are answered below.

You may register individually or in a team of two to four. Only one member of a team needs to sign up.

Individual applicants and incomplete teams may be matched with other incomplete teams or individuals.

Individual and team play start with the same amount of cash and have no fundamental differences.
